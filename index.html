About Me

I am Weihao Cheng (程炜豪), an undergraduate student at Hangzhou Dianzi University (HDU), affiliated with the School of Communication Engineering and the School of Computer Science and Technology.

My research focuses on medical image analysis, multimodal learning, and AI agents for healthcare. I am interested in building interpretable and reliable AI systems that connect clinical evidence with computational reasoning.

I am advised by Prof. Haicheng Tu, Prof. Huihui Ye (Intelligent Information Processing Laboratory), and Prof. Feiwei Qin (HDU-3DV-Lab).
