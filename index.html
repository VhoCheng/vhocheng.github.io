😉 About Me

🌱 Hi, I’m Weihao Cheng (程炜豪), an undergraduate student at the School of Communication Engineering, Hangzhou Dianzi University (HDU).

🔭 My research interests include magnetic resonance imaging, AI and medical imaging big data, multimodal learning, and AI agents for healthcare. I also work on intelligent big data analytics and smart energy (power system optimization).

📝 Editorial Service: Guest Editor, JoVE Topical Collection — Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging Diagnostics: Reproducible Protocols from Data Curation to Clinical Validation. Editor profile ↗

🔖 Academic Services: Conference reviewer for ICLR2027, MICCAI2026, NLPCC2026, CTESP2026, ICHI2026. MICCAI 2026 Honorable Mention, received during my sophomore year. Journal reviewer for WJGS, WJP, WJG.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Academic Communities: Student member of CCF, CIC, CMES, CAAI, CSIG, CSBME.
